## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `cinematic`
- Creative direction: Apple Music-level romantic product cinematography; intimate, monochrome, editorial
- Interpretation: Deep contrast, slow confident camera moves, crisp typography, and emotional resonance. High restraint: no generic SaaS badges or startup tropes.
- Angle: A deeply romantic product film highlighting the intimacy of two people listening to the exact same moment of a song, regardless of physical distance.
- Hook: "TWO PEOPLE. MILES APART. ONE SONG." with the breathing Cariño interlocking circles emblem.
- Outro: Cariño emblem + wordmark + "Together, apart." resolving peacefully.
- Avoid:
  - Generic SaaS language ("Boost your productivity", "revolutionary platform")
  - Abstract filler graphics
  - Cheap 2D flat cards with rainbow borders
  - Cluttered dashboard elements

# Brag Plan: Cariño

## What is this app?
Cariño is a private, real-time synchronized music listening room designed specifically for two people who are apart, delivering sub-millisecond audio synchronization and an intimate 3D album carousel experience.

## The angle
"Together, apart." This is an Apple Music-level romantic product launch film, not a generic SaaS feature demo. Every frame treats distance not as a disconnect, but as space bridged by music. It honors Cariño's exact design system: pure obsidian black (`#000000`), crisp white typography, minimal hairline borders, tactile room codes, and the iconic interlocking circles emblem.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
An ultra-minimal, high-contrast typography and emblem reveal in complete quiet elegance:
"TWO PEOPLE."
"MILES APART."
"ONE SONG."
The Cariño interlocking circles emblem emerges from the dark, breathing softly with an intimate warm glow.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Connection:** Room code `CARINO` enters with crisp tactile feedback. Presence indicators snap alive: *Anshuman* and *Cariño*, joined in private session.
- **The Realtime Engine:** The live sync indicator lights up with sub-millisecond precision: `Synced · 0ms drift` and authoritative audio unlock.
- **The 3D Music Experience:** The physical 3D Album Carousel floating in space with "Darmiyaan" by Rekha Bhardwaj & Raghav Kaushik, dynamic ambient blurred glow, and the floating minimal MiniPlayer.

## Outro / punchline
The music and visual energy resolve into pure editorial stillness:
The Cariño wordmark & interlocking emblem.
"Together, apart."
`listentocarino.vercel.app`

## User flow worth showing
1. **Entry:** Entering private room `CARINO` — two lovers connecting across distance.
2. **Action:** Starting playback of "Darmiyaan", the floating 3D carousel centering the track while audio unlocks and synchronizes instantly.
3. **Result:** Dual listening with `0ms drift`, seamless volume envelope, and shared intimate ambience.
